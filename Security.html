Cybersecurity Information

Cybersecurity is important because computers and online accounts can contain personal and private information.

Basic Cybersecurity Tips

  • Use strong passwords.
  • Do not reuse the same password for every account.
  • Use two-factor authentication when available.
  • Be careful when clicking links in emails.
  • Keep your computer and software updated.

Learning basic cybersecurity practices can help protect your computer, accounts, and personal information.
